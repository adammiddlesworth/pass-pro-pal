Paid status is expressed in GoHighLevel as tags on each synced contact:

- `passpal-paid` — user has at least one completed purchase
- `passpal-paid-<product_id>` — one tag per purchased product (e.g. `passpal-paid-real_estate_exam`)
- `passpal-free` — user has no completed purchase

Use GHL's Tag filters/conditions to segment paid vs free users and to build workflows per product. No custom field will be added.
